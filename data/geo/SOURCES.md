# Geo data sources

Generated by `npm run geo:build` from `scripts/geo/sources.lock.json`. Do not edit by hand.

Every `*.derived.json` file in `data/geo` (including `data/geo/districts`) and `data/zones` is derived data: simplified
artwork built from the sources below, not the original data. Each file says so in its
`derived` and `notice` fields. The raw downloads are kept unmodified in the gitignored cache
(`.cache/geo/`) and re-verified against the sha256 below on every build.

Common transformations: coordinates projected to local metres (projection `cc-enu-v1`,
origin 40.7, -73.98; `src/lib/geo/projection.ts`), rounded to 1 m and delta-encoded.
Parameters live in `data/config/geo.json`.

## NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (NYC Open Data)

### Building Footprints

- Publisher: NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI)
- Layer: <https://services6.arcgis.com/yG5s3afENB5iO9fj/arcgis/rest/services/BUILDING_view/FeatureServer/0/query> (the public BUILDING_view)
- ArcGIS item: <https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=870bf69e8a8044aea4488e564c0b4010>
- Catalogue page: <https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/5zhs-2jue> (to re-verify from a US network; it refused requests from the build machine)
- Field documentation: <https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/master/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md>
- Terms: NYC Open Data Terms of Use <https://www.nyc.gov/html/data/terms.html>
- NAME field not downloaded: the queries request `outFields=HEIGHT_ROOF` only, so no building name or other attribute ever reaches the cache, the derived files or the game.
- Filter: `FEATURE_CODE IN (2100,5100,1006) AND Shape__Area>=40` (buildings, buildings under
  construction, cantilevered buildings; no sheds or slivers), within each district circle's
  bounding box. Pages are kept byte for byte; their total must equal the count query.
- Used for: `geo/districts/<district>.<tier>.derived.json` (building masses per district and
  quality tier).
- Transformations: a footprint is kept when its centroid lies inside the district circle and
  outside every landmark exclusion rectangle in `landmarks/frames.derived.json` (derived in
  the same build: a Blender model's `grounds` from its lock's game-scale footprint, S07c, or
  else the kit model's `exclusion`); `HEIGHT_ROOF` feet × 0.3048, rounded to whole metres (missing or zero: 9 m);
  snapped to 1 m; then per tier:
  - low: Douglas-Peucker 4 m, footprints under 150 m² dropped, lower than 8 m dropped, heights in 6 m bins, touching footprints of one bin merged (holes kept; the area filter applies to the merged mass)
  - mid: Douglas-Peucker 2 m, footprints under 60 m² dropped, heights in 3 m bins, touching footprints of one bin merged (holes kept; the area filter applies to the merged mass)
  - high: Douglas-Peucker 1 m, footprints under 40 m² dropped, heights in 1 m bins, no merging
- Rings are stored relative to the district centre, delta-encoded, with heights as a separate
  column; the files hold numbers only past their header.

### LION street centerlines (landmark frames)

One query per street, each an exact `Street` match on the LION FeatureServer (release 26C),
reduced to the fields used and ordered by segment id so the bytes are stable.

- Used for: `landmarks/frames.derived.json` (where each landmark model stands and faces).
- Codes (LION data dictionary <https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/dcp/assets/files/pdf/data-tools/bytes/lion_metadata.pdf>): `SegmentTyp` B both, C connector,
  E entrance/exit ramp, F faux, G generic, R roadbed, T terminator, U undivided, S suppressed;
  `RW_TYPE` 1 street, 3 bridge, 9 ramp. A bridge uses only its main-roadway segment types
  and roadway types (data/landmarks/models.json `frame`), never ramps or connectors.
- Transformations: projected to local metres; bridges: the length-weighted principal axis
  of those segments, its two ends where the extreme vertices project onto it (each checked
  to lie on the land of the borough it spans, from the borough boundaries above), the
  heading from the first spanned borough to the second; other landmarks: the bearing of
  the nearest segment of the fronting street plus the model's face angle. Headings are
  rounded to 0.1°, points to whole metres. No street geometry is kept.
- The item's own notice: "The Department of City Planning make no representation as to the accuracy of the information or its suitability for any purposes. The Department and the City disclaim any liability for errors that may be contained herein."

### LION street centerlines (street props)

One paged envelope query per prop zone (each building district, plus the circles around
homes, venues and landmarks in `data/config/geo.json` `streets.zones`), reduced to the
fields used (`outFields=SegmentID,Street,StreetWidth_Min`) and ordered by segment id so the bytes are stable.

- Filter: `RW_TYPE=' 1' AND FeatureTyp='0' AND RB_Layer IN ('B','G')` (ordinary public streets, single centerline; no highways,
  bridges, tunnels, ramps, paths, private or paper streets, and no roadbeds of divided streets).
- Used for: `geo/streets.derived.json` (the chains street props stand on, mid and high tiers
  only).
- Transformations: segments de-duplicated by `SegmentID` and kept when their midpoint lies in
  a prop zone circle; cut wherever they come within 150 m of a memorial, or within a
  landmark's exclusion rectangle grown by 10 m, both grown by the street's half-width plus
  6 m; joined into chains by street name, width and continuity; Douglas-Peucker
  (1 m) between junctions, rounded to whole metres. `StreetWidth_Min` (feet, curb to
  curb; 30 ft when missing) becomes each chain's half-width to 0.5 m; chains at least
  60 ft wide are flagged wide; a side whose point 8 m beyond the curb lies in a park
  (`geo/parks.derived.json`) is flagged a park edge. No street name is kept.
- The item's own notice: "The Department of City Planning make no representation as to the accuracy of the information or its suitability for any purposes. The Department and the City disclaim any liability for errors that may be contained herein."
