# Geo data sources

Generated by `npm run geo:build` from `scripts/geo/sources.lock.json`. Do not edit by hand.

Every `*.derived.json` file in `data/geo` (including `data/geo/districts`) and `data/zones` is derived data: simplified
artwork built from the sources below, not the original data. Each file says so in its
`derived` and `notice` fields. The raw downloads are kept unmodified in the gitignored cache
(`.cache/geo/`) and re-verified against the sha256 below on every build.

Common transformations: coordinates projected to local metres (projection `cc-enu-v1`,
origin 40.7, -73.98; `src/lib/geo/projection.ts`), rounded to 1 m and delta-encoded.
Parameters live in `data/config/geo.json`.

- Terms: NYC Open Data Terms of Use <https://www.nyc.gov/html/data/terms.html>
- Downloaded: per community district (`CD`), the lot count, the sums of `BldgArea` and
  `LotArea` and the mean `NumFloors` (ArcGIS `outStatistics`). No lot geometry, address, owner

- Transformations: `CD` mapped to its CDTA zone (borough digit to MN/BX/BK/QN/SI); joint

  rotated per borough (manhattan 29°, bronx 0°, brooklyn 0°, queens 0°, staten-island 0°), seeded with `src/engine/rng.ts` (seed 4104). A point
  gets a block with chance min(1, FAR ÷ 3), 3.3 m per floor around the district's
  mean floors (±40%). Points in a district circle, a park or cemetery, a joint
  interest area or the water get none.

## NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (NYC Open Data)

### Building Footprints

- Publisher: NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI)
- Layer: <https://services6.arcgis.com/yG5s3afENB5iO9fj/arcgis/rest/services/BUILDING_view/FeatureServer/0/query> (the public BUILDING_view)
- ArcGIS item: <https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=870bf69e8a8044aea4488e564c0b4010>
- Catalogue page: <https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/5zhs-2jue> (to re-verify from a US network; it refused requests from the build machine)
- Field documentation: <https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/master/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md>
- Terms: NYC Open Data Terms of Use <https://www.nyc.gov/html/data/terms.html>
- NAME field not downloaded: the queries request `outFields=HEIGHT_ROOF` only, so no building name or other attribute ever reaches the cache, the derived files or the game.
- Filter: `FEATURE_CODE IN (2100,5100,1006) AND Shape__Area>=40` (buildings, buildings under
  construction, cantilevered buildings; no sheds or slivers), within each district circle's
  bounding box. Pages are kept byte for byte; their total must equal the count query.
- Used for: `geo/districts/<district>.<tier>.derived.json` (building masses per district and
  quality tier).
- Transformations: a footprint is kept when its centroid lies inside the district circle and
  outside every landmark exclusion rectangle in `data/landmarks/frames.derived.json` (when that
  file exists); `HEIGHT_ROOF` feet × 0.3048, rounded to whole metres (missing or zero: 9 m);
  snapped to 1 m; then per tier:
  - low: Douglas-Peucker 4 m, footprints under 150 m² dropped, lower than 8 m dropped, heights in 6 m bins, touching footprints of one bin merged (holes kept; the area filter applies to the merged mass)
  - mid: Douglas-Peucker 2 m, footprints under 60 m² dropped, heights in 3 m bins, touching footprints of one bin merged (holes kept; the area filter applies to the merged mass)
  - high: Douglas-Peucker 1 m, footprints under 40 m² dropped, heights in 1 m bins, no merging
- Rings are stored relative to the district centre, delta-encoded, with heights as a separate
  column; the files hold numbers only past their header.

- Publisher: Metropolitan Transportation Authority (MTA)
- Downloaded from: <https://rrgtfsfeeds.s3.amazonaws.com/gtfs_subway.zip>
- Catalogue page: <https://www.mta.info/developers>
- Retrieved: 2026-10-06
- File: `.cache/geo/mta-gtfs-subway.zip` (5615228 bytes, sha256 `2c9e69b82ac596a2fe660b56656509570cfba3c2e4c50be75eaf624eee1bc6f8`)
- Terms: MTA Developer Terms and Conditions (updated 2024-03-13) <https://www.mta.info/developers/terms-and-conditions>
- The raw zip is kept unmodified in the gitignored cache; it is read in memory and never
  extracted to disk.
- Used for: `geo/subway.derived.json` (lines and stations).
- Transformations: shapes projected to metres, Douglas-Peucker simplification
  (3 m), shapes dropped when they repeat a kept path within 5 m,
  rounded to whole metres; stations are GTFS parent stations with the lines whose trips stop
  there; route colours dropped (no MTA line bullets or colours are used).
- Terminal extensions (a route's shapes end more than 10 m short of a station it serves; at most 150 m):
- Line `n` extended 103 m to station `q05`
- Line `q` extended 103 m to station `q05`
- Line `r` extended 103 m to station `q05`
